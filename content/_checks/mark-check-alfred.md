## Wrong
1. **what-others-say/islam (how_to_respond)** says the ancient sources are "about six centuries earlier than the Qur'an." The Qur'an dates to c. 610-632. Tacitus (c. 116) is about 5 centuries earlier, and Paul (50s) about 5.7. Fix: "five to six centuries earlier."

## Contested
1. **islam view, and q25 explanation:** "every ancient source … affirms it" / "all ancient non-Muslim sources affirm it." Some 2nd-3rd-century Gnostic texts (Second Treatise of the Great Seth, Coptic Apocalypse of Peter) deny that Jesus truly suffered on the cross. Fix: "every first- and early second-century source that mentions his death, Christian, Jewish and Roman, affirms the crucifixion."
2. **late-anonymous short_answer:** "no rival names ever circulated." This is close to Hengel and Gathercole ("The Alleged Anonymity of the Canonical Gospels," JTS 2018), but it is still debated. Fix: "we know of no early tradition naming different authors."
3. **resurrection.md fact 1:** "Tacitus and Josephus confirm it." Josephus's mention of the crucifixion sits inside the Testimonium (Ant. 18.3.3), which was partly interpolated. Most scholars keep the Pilate/cross clause as authentic, but it is not certain. Fix: add "(in the core of a passage later touched up by copyists)."
4. **resurrection.md, "The question is not really the evidence…"** This is rhetorical overreach against the "never overclaim" rule. Fix: "We think the resurrection explains these facts best; part of the question is whether we allow that God might act."
