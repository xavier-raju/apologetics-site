- 53:8 'My people': Confirmed as an argument. Fair to add the usual reply in one clause (Israel-view readers take the speaker as the nations' kings).
- 49:5-6: Contested as worded. 49:3 names this same servant "Israel", so a Jewish friend will raise it at once. Add the Christian answer: the servant is the one true Israel who restores the nation (49:3 with 49:5-6).
- 53:9-12 sinless, dies, then lives: Confirmed. Add Matt 27:57-60 for the rich man's tomb.
- Sinless contrast (1:4; 42:24; 43:24): Contested on tone (house rule: nothing negative about Jewish people). Suggest framing it as everyone in Isaiah, the prophet included, confessing sin (Isa 6:5; 53:6 "us all"), with only the servant sinless.
- Card bullet on Targum Jonathan dropped John's fix that it applies the sufferings of ch. 53 to others; short_answer "The oldest Jewish readings also pointed to the Messiah" overstates for the same reason. Restore the caveat (the prophecy entry keeps it).
- For John: 'majority view only in the medieval period' should be squared with Origen, Contra Celsum 1.55 (c. AD 248), which reports Jews of his day reading the servant as the nation.
