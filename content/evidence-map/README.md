# Evidence map (content)

Owner: Luke. Checker: Matthew. Data only.

- `places.json`: 19 places with lat/lon, source dataset, source id and license. Coordinates come only from OpenBible.info Bible Geocoding Data (CC BY 4.0) and Pleiades (CC BY 3.0). The Jordan has no single point (null lat/lon). el-Araj and et-Tell are both shown as candidates for Bethsaida.
- `finds.json`: 15 finds. Each has `shows` and `does_not_prove`, sources and a status. `image_license` is null everywhere for now: no image is cleared yet.
- Silwan / City of David: the Pilgrimage Road and Pool of Siloam lie under the Palestinian neighborhood of Silwan and are dug with the ELAD (City of David) foundation. Keep that wording neutral and factual.
- Two finds seen on social media (a large ritual bath lifted whole, a stretch of Jerusalem's Lower Aqueduct) were not confirmed and are left out on purpose.
- The Megiddo mosaic has no point in our approved datasets yet (place_id null).
