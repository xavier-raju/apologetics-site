---
title: Can we know anything about Jesus?
minutes: 3
---
Historians judge ancient people by sources: how many, how early, how independent. Jesus has Paul's letters (50s), four Gospels (about AD 65 to 100, and many date some before 70), and non-Christian writers like Josephus, Tacitus, and Pliny.

That is a lot for a traveling teacher from a small province. It's why almost every historian, believing or not, agrees he lived and was crucified under Pilate.

**Takeaway:** Start with what nearly everyone agrees on. That's solid ground.
