---
title: Witnesses outside the Bible
minutes: 3
---
- **Josephus**, a Jewish historian (about AD 93), mentions James, "the brother of Jesus, who was called Christ" (Antiquities 20.9.1).
- **Tacitus**, a Roman historian (about AD 116), says Christ was executed by Pontius Pilate (Annals 15.44).
- **Pliny the Younger**, a Roman governor (about AD 112), says Christians met before dawn and recited "a form of prayer to Christ, as to a divinity": Letters 10.96 (X.97 in the Melmoth/Bosanquet numbering).

These writers weren't fans. That's what makes them useful.

**Honest note:** a longer Josephus passage was probably touched up by later Christian copyists. Most scholars think a shorter original mentioned Jesus.
