---
title: Was the Bible changed?
minutes: 4
---
We don't have the originals of any ancient book. We have copies. For the New Testament we have about 5,500 to 5,700 Greek manuscripts, more than any other ancient work.

Copies differ. Scholar Peter Gurry estimates about 500,000 variants, not counting spelling. That sounds scary until you see why: more copies means more chances to count differences. Most are spelling, word order, or slips.

Some passages were added later, and we say so: John 5:4, Mark 16:9-20, and John 7:53-8:11 are not in the earliest copies. Most Bibles mark them. No core belief depends on them.

**Takeaway:** Because we have so many copies, scholars can spot changes. That's a strength.
