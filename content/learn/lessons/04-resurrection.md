---
title: The resurrection in five facts
minutes: 4
---
Jesus died by crucifixion. His followers believed they saw him alive. Paul the persecutor changed. James the skeptic changed. The tomb was empty.

Gary Habermas argues most scholars accept these facts (his survey, his claim). The question is what best explains them. Theft, fainting, wrong tomb, and hallucinations each leave facts unexplained. We think "He is risen" explains them best.
