---
title: Answering with gentleness
minutes: 2
---
1 Peter 3:15 asks us to give a reason for our hope, and to "respond with gentleness and respect" (BSB).

- Listen first. Ask, "What makes you think that?"
- Say what's true in their point.
- Give one clear answer, not ten.
- Admit what's still debated. Honesty builds trust.
- Never mock. Pray for them.
