# Welcome, friend

We're glad you're here. This site is a free gift from our church family to yours. It's for anyone who wants to know why Christians believe Jesus lived, died, and rose again, and how to share that hope gently.

## What this site is

A place to explore the evidence for Jesus at your own pace. We make the case as strongly as the facts allow, and we tell you honestly where scholars still disagree. We would rather you trust us than be impressed by us.

## How to use it

- **Have a question?** Type it in the "Ask a question" box. It will point you to an answer we have checked.
- **Heard a hard objection?** Open "Answer an objection." Each card gives a 30-second answer, the evidence, and where to read more.
- **Want the heart of it?** Read "The resurrection." Everything rests there (1 Corinthians 15:14).
- **Want to grow?** Try "Learn mode," with short lessons and a quiz.
- **Talking with a friend of another faith?** "What others say" explains their view fairly and how to respond kindly.

## A word on how we speak

"But in your hearts sanctify Christ as Lord. Always be prepared to give a defense to everyone who asks you the reason for the hope that is in you. But respond with gentleness and respect," (1 Peter 3:15, BSB)

Answers matter. So does kindness. Win the person, not just the argument.

## Where our material comes from

Bible quotes come from the Berean Standard Bible (BSB), which is free to share. Books and articles by scholars are summarized in our own words and linked. Our research began with a public dossier by 0xSero, which we link to with thanks: https://github.com/0xSero/all-historical-evidence-of-jesus-christ
