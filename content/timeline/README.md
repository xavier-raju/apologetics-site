# Life of Jesus timeline (content)

Owner: Luke. Checker: Matthew. Data only; page code follows TARS's style guide.

- `events.json`: 34 events, birth (c. 6-4 BC) through the Frankfurt amulet (c. AD 230-270).
- Fields: id, title, date_display, date_start, date_end (negative = BC, no year zero), approximate, category (life, history, ministry, passion, resurrection, church, writings, witness, manuscript, archaeology), summary, scripture_refs, sources, status (Confirmed or Contested), related_places (ids in `../evidence-map/places.json`).
- Approximate dates are flagged with `approximate: true` and should be shown with "c." on the page. The crucifixion is shown as AD 30 or AD 33, since both are widely held.
- Scripture references are references only. Any quoted verse on the site must come from the Berean Standard Bible (public domain).
- All text is our own wording. 0xSero's dossier has no license, so it is linked, never copied.
