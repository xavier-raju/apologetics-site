# Outside witnesses

You might expect only Christians to write about Jesus. In fact, within about 150 years of the cross, Roman officials, a Jewish historian, a Greek satirist and hostile critics all mention him or his followers. None of them was trying to help the church. Several of them disliked Christians.

Taken together, they confirm the outline: Jesus was a real Jewish teacher, he was executed under Pontius Pilate while Tiberius was emperor, his followers spread quickly as far as Rome, and they worshipped him as divine very early.

They do not prove the resurrection, and we will not pretend they do. What they do is close the door on the claim that Jesus never existed. On each card we show the exact words, who wrote them, how long after the cross, what they show, and what they do not.

Dates count from a crucifixion in AD 30. If it was AD 33, take off three years.
