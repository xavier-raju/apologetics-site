Attribution

Much of this site rests on the research of 0xSero, whose report All Historical Evidence of Jesus Christ gathered the references, the life catalogue, the prophecy catalogue, the gospel registry and the dating ranges used in the explorers. The repo carries no licence, so we link to it and write everything in our own words. Thank you.

The "Why this matters" reflections on the resurrection fact cards were drafted by Gospel Bot, with every verse checked against the Berean Standard Bible. They are reflection, not evidence, and the theory tester never counts them.

Below is every source our team opened and confirmed. Sources we could not open are not listed and nothing rests on them.
