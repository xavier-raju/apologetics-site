Dating chart

How far each text sits from the events it describes.

  • Framingcreed in a letter· AD 30-33 (Lüdemann, quoted by Habermas 2006); mid-30s on the wider view (Habermas 2005)

    1 Corinthians 15:3-7 creed (received by Paul)

    The creed Paul quotes in 1 Corinthians 15 was handed to him, by his own account, in the years soon after the crucifixion.

    Go deeper

    Lüdemann, a sceptical scholar quoted by Habermas in Dialog 2006, dates the forming of the appearance traditions in 1 Corinthians 15:3-8 to between 30 and 33 CE. Habermas (2005) reports the broader majority view that Paul received this material in the mid-30s AD.

    • Gary R. Habermas, Experiences of the Risen Jesus: The Foundational Historical Issue in the Early Proclamation of the Resurrection, Dialog 45/3, 2006 · pp. 288-297
    • Gary R. Habermas, Resurrection Research from 1975 to the Present: What are Critical Scholars Saying?, Journal for the Study of the Historical Jesus 3.2, 2005 · pp. 135-153 (web copy, no page numbers)
