Life of Jesus

All 175 units of the repo's life catalogue, in order. Framing means the passages exist and tell this unit, not that history has proven it.

  • Framing1· Before the public ministry: theological origins, birth, and childhood

    Pre-existence and incarnation

    John's Gospel opens by calling Jesus the Word who was with God and became flesh.

    Jn 1:1-18

    Go deeper

    John 1 says the Word was in the beginning. Paul's letters to the Philippians and Colossians and the letter to the Hebrews also describe Christ as existing before his human life. Philippians 2 is often thought to quote an early hymn.

    • John 1, text and notes USCCB, New American Bible Revised Edition
  • Framing2· Before the public ministry: theological origins, birth, and childhood

    The genealogies

    Matthew and Luke each give a genealogy of Jesus.

    Mt 1:1-17 Lk 3:23-38

    Go deeper

    Matthew traces the line from Abraham forward. Luke traces it back to Adam. The names between David and Joseph differ, and ancient and modern writers have proposed different explanations.
