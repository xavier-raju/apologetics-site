The text we have

Numbers that hold up

about 5,500 to 5,700
About 5,500 to 5,700 handwritten Greek copies of all or part of the New Testament survive.
about 141
About 141 of these are papyri, the earliest group, mostly fragments.
about 500,000
Scholars estimate about 500,000 differences (variants) among the Greek manuscripts, not counting spelling.
