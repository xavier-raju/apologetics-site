A prophet like Moses

direct prediction

Old Testament · Deuteronomy 18:15-19

15 The LORD your God will raise up for you a prophet like me from among your brothers. You must listen to him.

16 This is what you asked of the LORD your God at Horeb on the day of the assembly, when you said, “Let us not hear the voice of the LORD our God or see this great fire anymore, so that we will not die!”

17 Then the LORD said to me, “They have spoken well.

18 I will raise up for them a prophet like you from among their brothers. I will put My words in his mouth, and he will tell them everything I command him.

19 And I will hold accountable anyone who does not listen to My words that the prophet speaks in My name.

Two readings, fairly stated

Moses promises that God will raise up a prophet like him from among Israel, and the people must listen to him. Peter and Stephen both apply this to Jesus, and crowds in John 6 wondered whether Jesus was 'the Prophet.' Deuteronomy 34:10 notes that no prophet like Moses had arisen yet.

Jewish readers generally take this as God's promise to keep sending true prophets, such as Joshua, Samuel or Jeremiah, so Israel would not need fortune-tellers. Maimonides taught that Moses' prophecy differed from that of every other prophet.

Note. This is about a role and an office, not a single detailed event.
