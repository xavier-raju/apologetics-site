Out of Egypt I called My Son

typological

Old Testament · Hosea 11:1

1 When Israel was a child, I loved him, and out of Egypt I called My son.

New Testament

13 When the Magi had gone, an angel of the Lord appeared to Joseph in a dream. “Get up!” he said. “Take the Child and His mother and flee to Egypt. Stay there until I tell you, for Herod is going to search for the Child to kill Him.”

14 So he got up, took the Child and His mother by night, and withdrew to Egypt,

15 where he stayed until the death of Herod. This fulfilled what the Lord had spoken through the prophet: “Out of Egypt I called My Son.”

The original words

Tap a word to see its meaning.

Two readings, fairly stated

Hosea looks back on the exodus, when God called his 'son' Israel out of Egypt. Matthew sees Jesus repeating Israel's story: he goes down to Egypt and comes back, passes through water, and is tested in the wilderness. Jesus is presented as the faithful Son where Israel struggled.

Jewish readers point out, correctly, that Hosea is speaking about Israel's past, not predicting a future person.

Note. Christians agree this is not a prediction. It is a classic example of typology, which was a recognized way of reading Scripture in Jesus' time. We present it honestly as typology.
