Prophecy explorer

Each passage: the Old Testament text, its New Testament fulfillment, the original words, and the traditional Jewish reading shown fairly beside the Christian one.

What the labels mean
Direct prediction
A forward-looking promise or oracle that Christians read as about the Messiah. The label describes the passage's form; who fulfils it may still be disputed (see 'contested').
Typological
A real person or event in its own setting that the New Testament presents as a foreshadowing of Jesus. Not a prediction on its surface.
Pattern
A broad promise or theme running through Scripture that the New Testament sees completed in Jesus, without quoting it as a one-to-one prediction.
