A voice crying in the wilderness

direct prediction

Old Testament · Isaiah 40:3

3 A voice of one calling: “Prepare the way for the LORD in the wilderness; make a straight highway for our God in the desert.

New Testament

1 In those days John the Baptist came, preaching in the wilderness of Judea

2 and saying, “Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is near.”

3 This is he who was spoken of through the prophet Isaiah: “A voice of one calling in the wilderness, ‘Prepare the way for the Lord, make straight paths for Him.’”

3 “A voice of one calling in the wilderness, ‘Prepare the way for the Lord, make straight paths for Him.’”

23 John replied in the words of Isaiah the prophet: “I am a voice of one calling in the wilderness, ‘Make straight the way for the Lord.’”

The original words

Tap a word to see its meaning.

Two readings, fairly stated

Isaiah hears a voice calling for a road to be prepared for the LORD. All four Gospels apply it to John the Baptist, who prepared the way for Jesus. That makes the coming of Jesus the coming of the LORD himself.

In context the voice announces God leading the exiles home from Babylon. The Dead Sea Scrolls community also read this verse as its own calling to prepare in the wilderness, which shows it was read as forward-looking around Jesus' time.

Note. The Gospel punctuation ('a voice in the wilderness') follows the Greek; the Hebrew reads 'in the wilderness prepare the way.'
