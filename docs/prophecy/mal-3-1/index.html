My messenger will prepare the way

direct prediction

Old Testament · Malachi 3:1

1 “Behold, I will send My messenger, who will prepare the way before Me. Then the Lord whom you seek will suddenly come to His temple—the Messenger of the covenant, in whom you delight—see, He is coming,” says the LORD of Hosts.

New Testament

2 As it is written in Isaiah the prophet: “Behold, I will send My messenger ahead of You, who will prepare Your way.”

10 This is the one about whom it is written: ‘Behold, I will send My messenger ahead of You, who will prepare Your way before You.’

27 This is the one about whom it is written: ‘Behold, I will send My messenger ahead of You, who will prepare Your way before You.’

Tap a word to see its meaning.

Two readings, fairly stated

God promises to send a messenger to prepare the way, after which the Lord will suddenly come to his temple. Jesus identifies John the Baptist as this messenger, and Mark opens his Gospel with this verse.

Jewish readers connect this with Malachi 4:5, where the messenger is Elijah, who is expected to return before the Day of the LORD. Elijah is still awaited, which is why a seat is set for him at the Passover meal.

Note. Jesus said John came 'in the spirit and power of Elijah' (Luke 1:17; Matthew 11:14), not that John was Elijah returned (John 1:21).
