Strike the shepherd, and the sheep will scatter

direct prediction

Old Testament · Zechariah 13:7

7 Awake, O sword, against My Shepherd, against the man who is My Companion, declares the LORD of Hosts. Strike the Shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered, and I will turn My hand against the little ones.

New Testament

31 Then Jesus said to them, “This very night you will all fall away on account of Me. For it is written: ‘I will strike the Shepherd, and the sheep of the flock will be scattered.’

27 Then Jesus said to them, “You will all fall away, for it is written: ‘I will strike the Shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered.’

The original words

Tap a word to see its meaning.

Two readings, fairly stated

On the way to Gethsemane, Jesus quotes this verse to warn that the disciples will scatter when he is arrested, which happens that night.

Jewish readers see a judgment on Israel's unworthy leaders, followed by the refining of a remnant (13:8-9).
