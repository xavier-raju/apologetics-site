Your King comes, riding on a donkey

direct prediction

Old Testament · Zechariah 9:9

9 Rejoice greatly, O Daughter of Zion! Shout in triumph, O Daughter of Jerusalem! See, your King comes to you, righteous and victorious, humble and riding on a donkey, on a colt, the foal of a donkey.

New Testament

4 This took place to fulfill what was spoken through the prophet:

5 “Say to the Daughter of Zion, ‘See, your King comes to you, gentle and riding on a donkey, on a colt, the foal of a donkey.’”

14 Finding a young donkey, Jesus sat on it, as it is written:

15 “Do not be afraid, O Daughter of Zion. See, your King is coming, seated on the colt of a donkey.”

16 At first His disciples did not understand these things, but after Jesus was glorified they remembered what had been done to Him, and they realized that these very things had also been written about Him.

Two readings, fairly stated

Zechariah pictures Jerusalem's king arriving humble and bringing peace, riding a donkey. Jesus chose to enter Jerusalem this way, and the crowds greeted him as the son of David. John notes that the disciples understood the connection only after the resurrection.

Jewish tradition also reads this as the Messiah's arrival. The Talmud (Sanhedrin 98a) contrasts coming 'with the clouds of heaven' (Daniel 7) with coming 'humble on a donkey' depending on whether Israel is worthy. The disagreement is over whether Jesus is that king, since Jewish readers expect the age of peace in the next verse (9:10) to come with him.

Note. Jesus' choice of a donkey was deliberate. Critics say this makes it staged; Christians answer that he was openly claiming the role.
