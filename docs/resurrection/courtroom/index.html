ContestedThe lawyer

Sir Lionel Luckhoo, the lawyer's verdict

Guyanese lawyer and judge (1914-1997) who became a Christian at 64 and wrote booklets arguing that Jesus rose from the dead.

Go deeper

Luckhoo was a Guyanese politician, diplomat and lawyer, later a judge. Wikipedia says he had a conversion at a meeting on 7 November 1978, then wrote apologetics booklets including 'The Question Answered: Did Jesus Rise from the Dead?'. It adds that Ross Clifford, Lee Strobel and Josh McDowell have written about his faith and apologetics.

Contested Wikipedia reports a 1990 Guinness listing of 245 consecutive murder acquittals, some on appeal.
